\section{Terminology: Risk \& Opportunity Response\, Monitoring\, and Control}

This section defines terminology related to how risks and opportunities are addressed.

\subsection{Risk \& Opportunity Response}
\label{sec:response-types}

%HD We are not using ATS!! MR: The definition of responses is included in the ATS Risk Software.

The following includes the definition of response plans to risks.

\begin{itemize}
 	\item \textbf{Avoid} ---
 	changing your strategy or plans to avoid the risk. This risk response strategy aims to remove the threat by any means. That can mean changing your management plan to avoid the risk because it’s detrimental to the project/program.

	\item \textbf{Transfer} ---
 	passing ownership and/or liability to a third party - Transfer or pass the work on, resolving the risk to a third party. For e.g. purchase fire insurance for an unfinished building.

 	\item \textbf{Mitigate} ---
 	Reducing the probability and/or impact of the risk below a threshold of acceptability - Some risks cannot be avoided and require action to reduce their impact. For e.g. work procedures and equipment designed to reduce workplace safety risks.
    
    Mitigations are captured in mitigation plans.

    When a Mitigation in implemented, the ticket is marked with a label "Mitigation\_implemented".

 	\item \textbf{Accept} ---
 	recognizing residual risks and devising responses to control and monitor them - This risk response strategy consists of identifying a risk, documenting all risk management information about it, and taking no action unless the risk is realized.
 \end{itemize}

The following includes the definition of response plans to opportunities.

\begin{itemize}
	\item \textbf{Exploit} ---
	Taking advantage of a risk to seize the opportunity it creates if the risk materializes.

	\item \textbf{Share} ---
	Sharing the risk among multiple stakeholders (teams/projects/programs)

	\item \textbf{Enhance} ---
	Enhancements are measures used to boost the chances of the opportunity happening.

	\item \textbf{Ignore} ---
	Opportunities that cannot be actively addressed by exploiting, sharing, or improving can be ignored without special measures.
\end{itemize}
